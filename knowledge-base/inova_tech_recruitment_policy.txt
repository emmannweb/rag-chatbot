# INOVA TECH ENTERPRISE RECRUITMENT AND HIRING POLICY
Document Version: 4.2
Effective Date: January 15, 2026
Applies To: All Global Operations, Human Resources, and Hiring Managers

---

## 1. PURPOSE AND OBJECTIVES
Inova Tech is committed to attracting, selecting, and retaining top-tier talent that aligns with our core values of innovation, integrity, collaboration, and excellence. The purpose of this policy is to establish a transparent, fair, and merit-based recruitment framework that ensures equal employment opportunities while securing the highest-caliber professionals to drive our technological advancements.

---

## 2. EQUAL EMPLOYMENT OPPORTUNITY (EEO) STATEMENT
Inova Tech is an Equal Opportunity Employer. We strictly prohibit discrimination or harassment of any kind based on race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, veteran status, disability, age, or any other status protected under applicable local and international laws. All employment decisions—including recruitment, hiring, promotion, and compensation—are based strictly on business needs, job requirements, and individual qualifications.

---

## 3. INTERNAL MOBILITY AND REFERRAL PROGRAMS
To foster continuous career growth and employee engagement, Inova Tech strongly prioritizes internal talent mobility. 
- **Internal Postings:** All open positions are published on the internal employee portal at least five business days prior to external advertisement. Employees who have completed a minimum of twelve (12) months in their current role are eligible to apply.
- **Employee Referral Bonus:** Inova Tech encourages staff to refer qualified external candidates. Successful hires through the employee referral program are eligible for a tiered referral bonus after the referred candidate completes a successful 90-day probationary review.

---

## 4. THE STANDARDIZED HIRING PROCESS
All recruitment processes across Inova Tech must adhere to a standardized multi-stage workflow to maintain fairness, predictability, and evaluation consistency:

1. **Requisition and Headcount Approval:** Hiring managers must submit a formal job requisition detailing budget allocation, role justification, and technical competency requirements, which must be approved by the Department Head and Human Resources.
2. **Sourcing and Screening:** HR and Talent Acquisition teams screen resumes against objective qualification matrices. Initial 30-minute phone screenings evaluate cultural alignment and baseline qualifications.
3. **Technical Assessment / Practical Evaluation:** Depending on the role, candidates undergo a role-specific technical assessment, take-home challenge, or portfolio review evaluated blindly to minimize unconscious bias.
4. **Structured Interview Rounds:** Candidates participate in a minimum of two structured interviews:
   - *Technical/Functional Panel:* Led by the hiring manager and senior team members to evaluate domain expertise and problem-solving methodologies.
   - *Culture Add Panel:* Led by cross-functional peers or leadership focusing on Inova Tech’s core behavioral competencies.
5. **Final Executive Review:** For senior and leadership positions, the Chief Executive Officer or relevant Vice President conducts a final alignment discussion.

---

## 5. OFFER EXTENSION AND ONBOARDING
- **Compensation Framework:** Salary offers are formulated using transparent market benchmarking data, internal equity audits, and candidate experience levels. 
- **Background Screening:** All offers of employment are contingent upon the successful completion of background checks, reference verifications, and compliance screenings as permitted by local labor laws.
- **Onboarding Journey:** Upon acceptance, new hires enter the 30-60-90 day structured onboarding program managed jointly by People Operations and designated departmental mentors.
